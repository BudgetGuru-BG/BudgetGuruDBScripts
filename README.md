# BudgetGuruDBScripts
DB Scripts For the BudgetGuru Application.

Managing personal finances on a day-to-day basis is often fragmented, time-consuming, and difficult to maintain consistently. Individuals make transactions across multiple bank accounts, cards, payment methods, and financial obligations, making it challenging to maintain a clear and accurate view of where their money is going.

Existing approaches often require users to manually record expenses or periodically review bank statements, receipts, emails, and account balances. Manual tracking creates significant friction and is prone to missed transactions, incorrect entries, and inconsistent categorization. At the same time, fully automated transaction tracking can reduce users' visibility and control over what information is ultimately recorded in their financial history.

Users therefore need a simple and reliable way to **capture, review, and manage their daily financial activity while retaining control over the accuracy of their records**.

The application aims to address this problem by providing a centralized personal finance tracking experience where users can:

* Record income and expenses manually as they occur.
* Upload bank statements to capture transactions without entering each transaction individually.
* Submit receipts through email so that transactions can be extracted and staged automatically.
* Review and approve staged transactions before they become part of the user's confirmed financial records.
* Track money flowing into and out of their accounts to better understand their day-to-day financial position.
* Track debts, outstanding balances, payment schedules, and repayment progress.
* Receive reminders for upcoming due dates and scheduled debt payments to reduce the likelihood of missed or late payments.

By combining **flexible transaction capture, assisted automation, user-controlled approval, daily cash-flow visibility, and debt management**, the application seeks to reduce the effort required to maintain accurate personal financial records and help users make more informed decisions about their money.
